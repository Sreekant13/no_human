The demo pipeline's guards all assert intent (the timing map, the recorder steps, the ffmpeg arguments) and none of them read the finished mp4 back, so a priming delay, a -shortest truncation or a stale narration track ships an artifact whose audio and picture disagree while the suite stays green. Producing the composed demo must verify the encoded result itself: prove every narration line has a speech onset at its mapped start, and that the picture shows the beat the narration names. A desynced artifact must fail the compose run, not ship.
