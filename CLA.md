**Version: 1.0**

This agreement is between you and eyalgolan, the maintainer of no_human ("the
Project"). You agree to it once, and it covers every contribution you make to
the Project from then on.

You record that agreement by adding one file to the
[`contributors/`](contributors/) directory in your first pull request, named
after your GitHub handle. That file is your signature: it names the version of
this document you agreed to, and git records who added it and when. CI refuses
a pull request whose commit authors have no entry.
[`contributors/README.md`](contributors/README.md) has the exact steps.

The version number above is what a ledger entry points at. A later revision of
this document gets a new version number, so an entry always names the terms
that were actually on the table when it was written.

If you are contributing on behalf of an employer, make sure someone who can
bind that employer has authorised it. See section 7.

Any work you deliberately submit to the Project for inclusion. Code, tests,
documentation, configuration, prompts, benchmark specs, issue text you ask to
have merged. A comment on an issue is not a contribution unless you mark it as
one.

## 3. Moral rights

Some countries — Germany and France among them — give an author rights that
survive a copyright licence and cannot be sold: the right to be named as the
author, and the right to object to a change to the work. Those rights would sit
awkwardly beside section 2. A refactor that rewrites your code, a release that
carries no per-file authorship, or a rename that drops your name from a header
are all ordinary things for this Project to do, and none of them should turn
into a claim.

So, to the fullest extent the law that applies to you allows it, you waive the
moral rights you hold in your contribution — including any right of attribution
and any right of integrity — and you agree not to assert them against the
maintainer, against anyone the maintainer licenses the software to, or against
anyone who receives it downstream.

Where the law does not allow a waiver, you agree not to enforce those rights
against those same people. This does not touch the copyright you keep under
section 2, and it does not stop the maintainer from crediting you.

If your employer owns your work, or has rights in it, then either:

- Get your employer's written permission, and say so in the pull request, or
- Have someone authorised to bind the employer agree to this document on the
  employer's behalf.

If you sign as an individual and your employer later asserts rights over the
contribution, tell the maintainer as soon as you know.

## 8. Housekeeping

You are not an employee, agent, or partner of the maintainer, and this
agreement does not make you one. You are owed nothing for contributing.

The maintainer is under no obligation to merge, keep, or ship any contribution.

If a court finds part of this agreement unenforceable, the rest still stands.

The maintainer may publish a revised version of this document, under a new
version number. A revision applies to contributions you submit after it is
published. It does not change the terms for contributions already merged, and
it does not change what an existing entry in [`contributors/`](contributors/)
means: that entry names the version it agreed to, and that is the version it
agreed to. If a revision is material, the maintainer will say so and ask for a
fresh entry.
